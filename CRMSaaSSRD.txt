About This Document


This Software Requirements Document (SRD) has been prepared as a pre-development reference for a proposed multi-tenant Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform. It is intended to help the client visualize the proposed product, understand the scope of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), and align on functional and technical expectations before development begins.
This is a proposal and planning document, not a technical development guide. Detailed engineering specifications, API contracts, and database schemas will be produced separately once the scope described here is approved.


1. Executive Summary


This document presents the proposed design and scope for a multi-tenant Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, delivered as Software-as-a-Service (SaaS). The platform is designed to help small and medium-sized businesses manage clients, appointments, inventory, and quotations from a single, centralized, cloud-based system.
The platform will be built as a multi-tenant SaaS solution, meaning a single deployment of the software will securely serve multiple independent companies (tenants) at once. Each company that subscribes to the platform will operate within its own isolated workspace, with its own users, clients, appointments, and data — completely separated from every other company using the system.
The scope defined in this document represents the Minimum Viable Product (MVP): a carefully selected set of core business features that deliver immediate operational value to a business owner and their team. The MVP has been deliberately scoped to avoid unnecessary complexity in the first release, while ensuring the underlying architecture is built to expand smoothly into a broader, more feature-rich CRM platform over time.
The purpose of this document is to give the client a clear, complete, and professional overview of what will be delivered in the MVP, how the platform will be structured, and how it can evolve in future phases — before any development work begins.


2. Product Goals


The CRM platform is guided by the following core product goals:
* Provide a simple, centralized system for managing client relationships, appointments, and inventory in one place.
* Deliver a true multi-tenant SaaS architecture that allows the product to be offered to many independent businesses without duplicating infrastructure.
* Keep the MVP lean and focused on core operational workflows, avoiding feature bloat that could delay time-to-market.
* Design the platform so that new modules (payments, messaging, analytics, mobile apps) can be added later without re-architecting the system.
* Ensure strict data isolation and security between tenants so that every business's data remains private and protected.
* Offer an interface that is intuitive enough for non-technical staff (business owners, sales representatives) to use with minimal training.
* Support responsive access from desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers from day one, without requiring a native mobile application in the first release.


3. Target Users


The platform is designed around four primary user types, each with distinct goals and levels of access. Understanding these personas ensures the interface and permissions are tailored to real day-to-day usage.
3.1 Business Owner
The Business Owner is the primary decision-maker for a subscribing company. They set up the company workspace, invite staff, configure company settings, and monitor overall performance through dashboards and reports. They need visibility across the entire business without necessarily performing day-to-day data entry themselves.
3.2 Staff
Staff members are operational users responsible for day-to-day tasks such as managing clients, scheduling appointments, and updating inventory. They typically have access limited to the modules relevant to their role and do not manage company-wide settings or billing.
3.3 Sales Representative
Sales Representatives focus on client acquisition and relationship management. Their primary tools are the client management and quotation modules, allowing them to create and track quotations, follow up with prospects, and convert opportunities into confirmed business.
3.4 Administrator (Super Admin)
The Super Admin is not a member of any individual client company — this role belongs to the team operating the SaaS platform itself. The Super Admin oversees all tenants (companies) on the platform, manages platform-wide configuration, and ensures the system runs smoothly across all customers.

	4. SaaS Architecture Overview


The platform is designed from the ground up as a multi-tenant SaaS product. This section explains, at a conceptual level, the architectural principles that make the platform secure, scalable, and suitable for serving many independent businesses from a single system.
4.1 Multi-Tenant Architecture
A single instance of the application will serve multiple companies (tenants) simultaneously. Each tenant's data — clients, appointments, inventory, quotations, and settings — is logically separated from every other tenant's data using a dedicated tenant identifier associated with every database record. This approach allows the platform to scale efficiently while keeping infrastructure and maintenance costs manageable as the customer base grows.
4.2 Platform & Tenant Hierarchy
The platform operates on a clear three-level hierarchy that governs how the system, each business, and each individual user relate to one another. This hierarchy is the backbone of both the multi-tenant architecture and the permission system described throughout this document.
* Super Admin (Platform Owner) — sits above all tenants and operates the SaaS platform itself. The Super Admin provisions new tenant companies, monitors platform health, and manages global configuration, but does not participate in any individual company's day-to-day business data.
* Business Owner (Tenant Admin) — sits at the top of a single tenant (company). The Business Owner administers their own company's workspace: configuring settings, inviting and managing Business Users, and retaining full visibility over that company's data.
* Business Users / Staff — operate within a tenant under permissions granted by the Business Owner. This level includes both general Staff and Sales Representatives, each scoped to the modules and records relevant to their role, as detailed in Section 5.
This hierarchy exists specifically to reinforce tenant isolation and secure access: a Super Admin cannot casually browse a tenant's client list, a Business Owner cannot see other companies' data, and Business Users only ever operate inside the single tenant they belong to. Every login, session, and API request carries both an identity (which user) and a tenant context (which company), and the system enforces both before returning or modifying any data. The relationship between these three levels is illustrated in Figure 4 (Appendix B).
4.3 Secure Authentication
Every user, regardless of role, authenticates through a secure login system. Authentication tokens are used to identify both the user and the company (tenant) they belong to, ensuring that every request to the system is automatically scoped to the correct company's data.
4.4 User Roles & Permissions
Access to features and data is governed by a role-based permission system. Each user is assigned a role (Super Admin, Business Owner, or Staff/Sales Representative) that determines which modules, actions, and records they can access. This ensures that sensitive actions, such as managing company settings or viewing platform-wide data, are restricted to authorized users only.
4.5 Separate Data for Every Company
Data isolation is a foundational principle of the platform. No company can view, edit, or otherwise access another company's clients, appointments, inventory, or quotations. This isolation is enforced consistently at the application and database level for every request, in direct support of the hierarchy described in Section 4.2.
4.6 Cloud Deployment
The platform will be deployed on cloud infrastructure, allowing it to be accessed securely from anywhere with an internet connection. Cloud deployment also enables automated backups, monitoring, and simplified maintenance without requiring on-premise servers for any client.
4.7 Scalability
The architecture is designed to scale horizontally as the number of tenants and users grows. Application servers, database resources, and storage can be scaled independently based on demand, ensuring consistent performance as the customer base expands.


4.8 Tenant URL Structure
Each tenant company is addressed through a directory-based URL path rather than a subdomain. For example, a company named “Business1” would access the platform at www.wiztik.eu/business1, rather than through a subdomain such as business1.wiztik.eu.
This directory-based routing approach was selected for the MVP because it simplifies SSL certificate management (a single certificate covers the whole platform), avoids the additional DNS configuration overhead that subdomain-per-tenant routing requires, and still gives every business a clean, distinct, shareable web address. The application layer resolves the tenant from the URL path at the start of every request and applies the same tenant-scoping and isolation rules described in Section 4.5.

5. User Roles & Permissions


Access within the platform is controlled through three primary role levels, corresponding directly to the Super Admin → Business Owner → Business User/Staff hierarchy introduced in Section 4.2. This section defines the responsibilities and permissions of each role in more detail. Note that 'Staff' below also encompasses the Sales Representative persona introduced in Section 3, which is treated as a permission variant of the Staff/Business User role rather than a fully separate role type.
5.1 Super Admin
The Super Admin operates at the platform level, above all individual tenant companies. This role is used exclusively by the team operating the SaaS product.
* Create, suspend, or remove tenant (company) accounts on the platform.
* View platform-wide usage metrics and system health.
* Manage global platform settings and configuration.
* Access support tools to assist tenant companies when needed.
* Cannot directly access or modify a tenant's business data (clients, appointments, inventory) under normal operation, preserving tenant data privacy.


5.2 Business Owner
The Business Owner is the primary administrator within a single tenant company. This role has full control over that company's workspace.
* Manage company profile, localization, and language settings.
* Invite, edit, and remove staff and sales representative accounts within their company.
* Access all modules: Dashboard, Client Management, Appointments, Products & Stock, Quotations, Notifications, and Reports.
* View full company-wide reports and analytics.
* Configure company-level notification preferences.


5.3 Staff (including Sales Representative)
Staff accounts are operational users created by the Business Owner. Permissions can be scoped narrowly depending on the specific responsibilities assigned.
* Add, edit, and view clients assigned to them or shared across the company (configurable by the Business Owner).
* Create and manage appointments, including scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation.
* View and update product and inventory information, depending on permission level.
* Create and send quotations to clients (primarily used by Sales Representatives).
* Receive relevant notifications and reminders.
* Cannot access company-wide settings, billing information, or manage other user accounts.

6.1 Dashboard
Overview Dashboard
The Dashboard is the landing page after login. It gives each user a quick, role-appropriate snapshot of what matters most — clients, upcoming appointments, stock alerts, and recent activity — without needing to navigate into individual modules.
Main Functionality:
* Summary cards for total clients, upcoming appointments, and low-stock items.
* Quick links to recently viewed clients and pending quotations.
* A calendar widget on the main dashboard surfacing upcoming appointments, so staff can see what is coming next without opening the full Calendar module.
MVP Status: Included in MVP
Role-Based Dashboards & KPI Visibility
Rather than showing every user the same screen, the Dashboard adapts both its layout and its data according to the viewer's role, reflecting the platform's tenant and permission hierarchy described in Section 4.2.
Main Functionality:
* Business Owners see a company-wide dashboard: aggregated KPIs across all staff, all clients, and all appointments, plus company-level charts and reports.
* Staff and Sales Representatives see a personal/assigned dashboard: their own clients, their own upcoming appointments, and their own open quotations, without visibility into company-wide figures unless explicitly granted.
* Super Admins see a platform-level dashboard focused on tenant health and usage, not the operational data of any individual business.
* KPI visibility follows the same permission rules as the underlying modules — a metric is only shown to a user if they are also permitted to see the records behind it.

6.5 Quotations
Create Quotation
Allows staff and sales representatives to build a formal price quotation for a client, drawing from the product/service inventory.
Main Functionality:
* Select a client and add one or more products/services with quantities and pricing, drawn from the relevant warehouse/stock location.
* Automatically calculate subtotal for the quotation.
MVP Status: Included in MVP
Send Quotation
Delivers the completed quotation to the client for their review.
Main Functionality:
* Generate a shareable/viewable version of the quotation.
* Mark the quotation as Sent once delivered.
MVP Status: Included in MVP
Manage Quotation Status
Tracks the client's response and the overall progress of each quotation through a clearly defined set of statuses.
Main Functionality:
* Statuses include Draft, Sent, Pending Approval, Accepted, Rejected, and Expired.
* View all quotations filtered by status or client.
* Status history is retained on the quotation record for full traceability.
MVP Status: Included in MVP
Configurable Approval Workflow
Not every business wants a quotation to go out — or to be treated as final — the moment it is created. The platform supports a configurable internal approval step so each business can match the workflow to its own policies.
Main Functionality:
* Business Owners can enable or disable an internal approval step before a quotation moves from Draft to Sent.
* When enabled, a quotation created by Staff or a Sales Representative enters a Pending Approval status and must be approved by a Business Owner (or another authorized approver) before it can be sent to the client.
* Businesses that do not require this control can leave approval disabled, allowing quotations to move directly from Draft to Sent.
* The approval requirement, and who is authorized to approve, is configured per business, consistent with the platform's business-specific configuration approach described in Sections 6.6 and 6.7.
MVP Status: Included in MVP
Quotation Approval Impact on Stock
Because quotations reference specific products and quantities, their status has a direct relationship with inventory. This relationship is handled deliberately so that stock figures stay accurate without being locked up unnecessarily.
Main Functionality:
* While a quotation is in Draft or Pending Approval, referenced stock is not deducted or reserved — it remains fully available for other quotations or appointments.
* Once a quotation is Accepted (and, where enabled, has passed the approval step), the corresponding stock quantities are deducted from the relevant warehouse/location, in line with the Stock Updates feature described in Section 6.4.
* If an Accepted quotation is later cancelled or reversed, the stock movement can be manually corrected using the same Stock Updates functionality, with the adjustment recorded for audit purposes.
MVP Status: Included in MVP

7. Mobile Experience


The MVP will be delivered as a fully responsive web application, meaning every screen — dashboard, client management, appointments, inventory, and quotations — will adapt cleanly to desktop, tablet, and mobile browser widths without requiring a separate application.
This matters in practice because staff and sales representatives do not always work from a desk. A Sales Representative may check a client's history on a laptop before a visit, review an appointment on a tablet while in a client's office, and confirm a quotation from a phone immediately after a meeting. The responsive design ensures the same core workflows — viewing and updating clients, checking the calendar, reviewing quotations — remain fully usable across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile devices, so staff are not blocked from acting on information simply because they are away from a desktop computer.
This approach allows business owners and staff to access the platform from any device with a modern web browser from day one, without the additional time and cost of building and maintaining native mobile applications during the initial release.
Native Android and iOS applications are a natural and planned future enhancement (see Section 9: Future Features). Because the platform's backend is designed as a set of well-structured services from the outset, native apps can be introduced later without requiring changes to the core business logic.


8. Security


Security is treated as a foundational requirement rather than an add-on feature. The following measures will be built into the platform from the first release:
8.1 Authentication
All users authenticate using secure credential-based login. Session tokens are used to validate ongoing access, and sessions can be configured to expire after a period of inactivity.
8.2 Password Encryption
User passwords are never stored in plain text. Passwords are hashed using a strong, industry-standard, one-way hashing algorithm before being stored, so that even in the unlikely event of a data exposure, original passwords cannot be recovered.
8.3 Role-Based Access Control
Every action within the platform is checked against the permissions associated with the requesting user's role, as detailed in Section 5. This prevents users from accessing modules or data outside their authorized scope.
8.4 Secure APIs
All communication between the application interface and backend services occurs through authenticated API requests. Every request is validated for both user identity and tenant context before any data is returned or modified.
8.5 Data Isolation Between Tenants
Every database record is associated with a specific tenant (company). Application logic enforces that a query can never return or modify data belonging to a different tenant, regardless of the requesting user's role.
8.6 HTTPS
All traffic between users and the platform is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, protecting login credentials and business data from interception.


9. Future Features


The MVP has been deliberately scoped to deliver core value quickly while establishing a foundation for future growth. The following features are strong candidates for future phases, once the MVP has been validated with real users:
* Native mobile applications for Android and iOS.
* Push notifications for real-time alerts on mobile devices.
* An AI-powered assistant to help staff draft messages, summarize client history, or suggest follow-ups.
* Integrated payment processing for invoices and deposits.
* Automated invoice generation linked to accepted quotations.
* WhatsApp integration for client communication and reminders.
* SMS notifications as an alternative or complement to email.
* Advanced analytics and business intelligence dashboards.
* Marketing automation, including campaigns and client segmentation.
These items are listed for planning purposes only and are explicitly excluded from the MVP scope described in this document (see Section 12: MVP Scope).

10.1 Deployment & Hosting Recommendation
Hosting is one of the few decisions where the right answer depends heavily on the business's expected scale and budget at launch, rather than on technical merit alone. The table below compares three realistic options for deploying the MVP.
Option
	Characteristics
	Best Suited For
	AWS (Amazon Web Services)
	High scalability and global reach; auto-scaling, managed database, and monitoring services are all readily available; billing scales with usage, which typically means a higher baseline cost.
	A platform that has already validated demand and expects rapid growth in tenants or traffic.
	VPS (e.g., Hostinger or similar)
	A dedicated virtual server with predictable, fixed monthly cost; full control over the environment; scaling is manual (resizing the server or adding servers) rather than automatic.
	Recommended for the MVP — it comfortably supports the expected initial load at a controlled, predictable cost, while remaining flexible enough to migrate to AWS later if growth requires it.
	Shared Hosting
	Lowest cost option; resources are shared with other tenants of the hosting provider, with limited control over server configuration and weaker isolation guarantees.
	Not recommended for this platform: the multi-tenant SaaS architecture, background jobs (notifications/reminders), and data isolation requirements described in Section 4 and Section 8 exceed what shared hosting environments reliably support.
	

For the MVP, a VPS-based deployment is the recommended starting point. It keeps hosting costs predictable while the product is validated with real customers, and because the application is built against standard PostgreSQL and cloud object storage interfaces (Section 10), migrating to AWS at a later stage — once tenant volume justifies it — will not require re-architecting the application itself.

12. MVP Scope


To ensure complete clarity before development begins, the table below explicitly separates what is included in the MVP from what is deferred to future phases.
12.1 Included in MVP
* Multi-tenant SaaS architecture with a clear Super Admin → Business Owner → Business User/Staff hierarchy, secure access, and isolated company data.
* Directory-based tenant URL routing (e.g., www.wiztik.eu/business1).
* Role-based access control for Super Admin, Business Owner, and Staff (including Sales Representative permissions).
* Role-based dashboards with company-wide or personal KPI visibility depending on the viewer's role.
* Full client management: add, edit, search, notes, and client history, including business-defined customizable client fields.
* Complete client contact history covering meetings, calls, notes, and emails, with configurable outcome categories per business.
* Full appointment management: creation from calendar or client profile, scheduling, status tracking, rescheduling, cancellation, and full appointment history.
* Products & stock management: inventory, categories, stock updates, and availability, across multiple warehouses/stock locations.
* Quotations: creation, sending, status management, and a configurable internal approval workflow with defined stock impact on acceptance.
* Notifications: email notifications, appointment reminders, and follow-up reminders, independently configurable per business.
* Reports: client count, appointment statistics, and inventory summary, scoped and configurable per business.
* Settings: company profile, localization, and multi-language support with the ability to add further languages later.
* Fully responsive web experience across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
* Core security measures: authentication, password encryption, role-based access control, secure APIs, tenant data isolation, and HTTPS.


12.2 Not Included in MVP
* Native Android and iOS mobile applications.
* Push notifications.
* AI-powered assistant features.
* Integrated payment processing.
* Automated invoice generation.
* WhatsApp integration.
* SMS notifications.
* Advanced analytics and business intelligence dashboards.
* Marketing automation and campaign management.


Items listed as 'Not Included in MVP' are not rejected — they are intentionally deferred so that the initial release can be delivered efficiently and validated with real usage before additional investment is made. All items in this list are elaborated upon in Section 9: Future Features.




13. Conclusion


This document has outlined a focused, achievable MVP for a multi-tenant CRM SaaS platform — one that gives a business owner and their team everything they need to manage clients, appointments, inventory, and quotations from day one, while operating on an architecture built for long-term growth.
By scoping the MVP deliberately around core operational workflows, the platform can be brought to market efficiently, validated with real customers, and then expanded thoughtfully. The multi-tenant foundation, role-based permissions, and clean data model described in this document ensure that future enhancements — mobile apps, payments, messaging integrations, and advanced analytics — can be layered on without requiring a rebuild of the core system.
With this MVP as its foundation, the platform is well-positioned to gradually evolve into a complete, feature-rich CRM solution comparable in scope to established products in this space, while having been built specifically around the workflows and priorities most relevant to its initial customers.
